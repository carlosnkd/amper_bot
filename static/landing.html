Coddy

A chat assistant built on CrewAI, FastAPI, and Anthropic's Claude models. A three-agent pipeline — Planner, Coder, Reviewer — turns a short feature ticket into an editable plan, a real implementation, and a pass/fail review that sends the work back for another attempt when it fails. Chit-chat and one-off snippets are answered directly, skipping the pipeline entirely.
